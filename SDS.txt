Car Rental System
Chris E, Kevin P, Tigist T, Michelle V, Light O

Description
//idk if we're supposed to copy and paste the system overview from the document or summarize ourselves so..

This is a car rental system designed to manage a car rental company. This system will allow customers to rent vehicles both online and via a mobile app provided they have signed up for an account with the company. This system will also facilitate the distribution and signing of rental agreement contracts under secure conditions.

Employees will also be able to access the system in order to look up customers. Upon looking up a customer on the system, they will be able to see the rental status and contracts, check what cars are currently available, an update the status of cars.
